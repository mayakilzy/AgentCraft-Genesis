## Truthful Classification of the Limitation

Per Section 16: "If external provider cannot be used: do NOT fake the
evidence. Report REAL_PROVIDER_HARDENING_EVIDENCE = UNAVAILABLE and
classify the remaining limitation truthfully."

The hardening probes that COULD NOT be run against a real provider:

1. **False-success resistance under real LLM (P0-1, H-06):** The
   `hash-match` verification check is unit-tested with a stub computer
   that stages known content. A real-LLM probe would have a worker
   produce content for a mission with a known gold answer and verify
   that the hash-match check catches any deviation. The unit test
   proves the CHECK works correctly; the real-LLM probe would prove
   the WORKER produces content the check can meaningfully hash-compare.
   The check itself is provider-agnostic; the missing probe is the
   end-to-end real-LLM-to-hash-check path.

2. **Worker-instance epistemic isolation under real provider (P0-3):**
   The `ScopeableReasoningProvider.forInstance()` contract is verified
   under the development fallback (TASK-022A). A real-LLM probe would
   construct two workers with different instance keys, give each a
   different piece of information, and verify that worker B cannot
   recall worker A's information. Real-LLM providers are stateless by
   design (each API call is independent), so the contract is
   structurally satisfied; the missing probe is the empirical
   confirmation under a specific provider's actual behavior.

3. **Provider failure handling (P1 H-01, H-03):** The worker retry
   loop is unit-tested with a scripted provider that throws
   `ECONNRESET` and `AbortError`. A real-LLM probe would induce a
   real 429 rate-limit or network timeout and verify the retry loop
   recovers. The unit test proves the RETRY LOGIC works correctly;
   the missing probe is the end-to-end real-provider-to-recovery path.
